About

Designing presence, not just screens.

Design + Informatics @ UT Austin

I'm Dhatri, a designer interested in immersive media, product design, and data visualization. My work involves exploring how data can make more compelling and tangible visual experiences. I'm drawn to emerging technologies not just for their novelty, but for the new ways of experiencing and interacting with information in three-dimensional, embodied space. I bring a foundation in visual arts which feeds into how I see colors, composition, and form in environments, grounding my experience design in a strong visual sensibility.
