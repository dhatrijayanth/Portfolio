Context

Context and challenge

Visitors to the Texas Immersive Institute include students, faculty, and guests who may know little about prior projects. The wall had to orient them quickly and invite longer looking.

Content curation mattered as much as interaction. The selection had to feel representative without overwhelming the space.

The challenge was to make past immersive work feel present again, in a form people could walk up to and explore.

Goals

Goals

  1. Help first-time visitors understand what the Institute makes.
  2. Showcase past projects as an experience, not only as a list.
  3. Keep authorship clear: my contributions versus team contributions.
